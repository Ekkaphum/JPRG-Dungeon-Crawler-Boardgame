Human-readable log of changes to this project, newest first. Add an entry here whenever you commit — whether the change was made by Claude Code or by hand — so anyone picking up the project can see what happened without digging through `git log`.

## 2026-08-15

- **v0.3.12 — unique boss arenas and animated 16-bit boss sprites**
  - Replaced the shared battle backdrop with a dedicated dungeon arena for each boss: Ragorath's burning iron colosseum, Somnivar's moonlit dream observatory, and Aurelius's ruined white-gold throne hall.
  - Replaced the painted boss card on the battle stage with a four-frame × three-row 16-bit sprite sheet for every boss. Each continuously idles, then plays either its attack or spellcasting row when the paced `BOSS_MOVE` event appears.
  - Converted the approved sprite drafts to genuine transparent assets, preserved painted boss art in detail/tutorial/result screens, and added reduced-motion handling plus focused move-to-animation-row tests.
  - `npm test` 203/203 passing, `npm run typecheck` clean, production build successful, and the battle stage visually verified at desktop and 390 × 844 mobile viewports.

- **v0.3.11 — boss pass: three mechanics brought back to life at unchanged difficulty** — the heroes were rebuilt repeatedly from v0.3.3 onward while the bosses were never revisited, and measuring found real drift. Every change below was simulated in isolation against a captured baseline, and each one that moved difficulty was compensated with boss HP so the *character* of a fight changed without its *power* doing so. Baseline and final, 2,500 games: medium 91.8% → 92.4% win rate, hard 65.9% → 66.4%; per-boss clear (hard) Ragorath 83.3% → 83.6%, Somnivar 79.5% → 78.4%, Aurelius 65.9% → 66.4%.
  - **Somnivar's sleep tax was effectively dead — 7.8% reach — and is now scaled.** The v0.3.3 skill rebuild made almost the whole roster faster, leaving only 2 of 16 skills at the old ⏱≥5 threshold (Guard and Meteor). It now costs **+1 slot at ⏱4-5 and +2 at ⏱6+**, reaching 8 of 16 skills (~46% of declares). A flat "+2 at ⏱≥4" was tried first and was catastrophic — hard win rate fell 65.9% → **29.3%**, which is the clearest measurement yet of how tight this clock is: adding one slot to half the party's actions costs roughly 15pp on its own. **Somnivar HP 96 → 76** compensates, landing his clear rate within 1.1pp of baseline.
  - **Aurelius's Golden Throne is now ⚡ immediate**, the boss-side counterpart of the heroes' immediate skills via a new `BossMoveDef.immediate`. A king does not announce that he is ascending his throne. Chosen precisely because it deals no damage: a declared boss move is the most important public information in the ruleset (Guard, Trap! and Heal are all timed off it), so making one immediate deletes that read — and here there was no defensive reaction to lose. He now gets the move off ~25% more often and holds more armour (1.40 → 1.71 at battle end).
  - **Ragorath's Frenzy was tried as immediate and reverted**, which surfaced a genuinely interesting interaction: Rage resets when the boss resolves its own action, and an immediate move fires *immediately after* that reset — so it always lands at +0 Rage. Measured damage per use went **down**, 9.3 → 7.8, the opposite of the intent. The deeper reading is that Rage is a mechanic that specifically rewards the delay: the longer the party takes, the angrier he gets. Ragorath is therefore correct as a fully telegraphed boss and gets no immediate move.
  - **Aurelius's Procession now pierces Blessing.** It exists as the catch-up mechanic — it hunts whoever leads on points — but was landing only **6.9 of its printed 12** because party mitigation absorbed the rest; it now lands 9.1. Deliberately narrow: it ignores the party-wide reduction only, so Guard still redirects it and personal shields still absorb it. **Aurelius HP 106 → 96** compensates.
  - **Somnivar's Eternal Slumber was a move players almost never saw** — 0.18 uses per game at ⏱8. Reduced to **⏱6**, now ~0.25. Left otherwise untouched: an "also cancels your declared action" payload was considered and dropped for removing player agency in a way the rest of the ruleset never does.
  - `npm test` 201/201 passing (6 new tests covering the immediate-move contract, the pawn still walking its full ⏱, delayed moves being unaffected, Procession's pierce, and every other Aurelius move still respecting mitigation), `npm run typecheck` clean, `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` updated.

- **v0.3.9 — Trap! delays the boss instead of cancelling it, and board-game mode is frozen**
  - **Trap! now pushes the boss's declared move back 2 clock slots** (`TRAP_DELAY_SLOTS` in `src/engine/clock/skills.ts`) rather than deleting it. The move still lands; the boss just has to wait for it. The boss *pawn* moves with the delayed move, and because `walk.ts` builds each tick's visit queue **after** traps are processed, that pawn move also takes the boss out of the current queue — so it stalls for those slots. Worth spelling out because the old cancel did **not** cost the boss any tempo: its pawn never moved, so it stayed in the queue and simply declared a fresh move on the spot, losing only that one specific move. The new version is the opposite trade — the damage still arrives, but the boss genuinely loses turns.
  - **Measured as a wash, and that was the point of measuring**: 3,000-game sims put win rate at 65.7% (cancel) vs 66.0% (delay) and total boss damage at 246,065 vs 247,678 — both inside noise (±0.86pp standard error at this sample size). A trap only springs ~0.14 times per game, so no rewrite of what it does can move aggregate balance; this is a change of *character* (tempo denial vs damage negation), not of power. Recorded so nobody re-measures it expecting a swing.
  - Skill text (both languages), `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` and `docs/RULINGS.md` updated to the new wording; the bots' trap valuation keeps its existing premium with the comment corrected, deliberately unchanged so behaviour still matches what was measured.
  - **Board-game (tabletop) visual mode disabled** — frozen, not deleted. Its ~200 lines of styling in `index.css` and every code path stay exactly where they are; a new `SELECTABLE_VISUAL_MODES` list in `src/session/persistence.ts` decides what the menu offers, the same pattern `CHAR_IDS` vs `ALL_CHAR_IDS` already uses for Dax/Mira. Re-enabling it later is that one line. The picker is hidden entirely while only one mode is selectable, and a saved setting pointing at a disabled mode migrates back to the default so an existing player doesn't return to a screen that has had no styling pass since ⚡ immediate skills, the ⚡ badges, passives, or the v0.3.7 score rebuild.
  - **Liora's Meteor deliberately left alone.** A selectable 5/6/7 wind-up (12/17/23 damage, cancelled if she takes damage mid-cast) was fully implemented and simulated, then reverted unshipped — see the analysis in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md`. Headline: **47–48% of all casts were cancelled**, so printed damage nearly doubling (13 → 23) left actual damage per declare flat (9.05 → 8.87); Meteor declares doubled as Liora got stuck re-casting; Fireball was abandoned (8,855 → 827 declares) which would have killed `vera2`; and altruistic-play win rate collapsed 92.0% → 73.1%.
  - `npm test` 193/193 passing (one new test proves the delayed move still resolves later rather than vanishing), `npm run typecheck` clean.

- **v0.3.8 — Kit/Liora follow-up tuning: competitive win share tightened to a 9.9pp spread** — three targeted changes on top of v0.3.7, each measured in isolation. Full numbers in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md`.
  - **Trap!'s trigger roll eased 6+ → 5+ at Lv1** (`rollBaseTarget`). Diagnosis first: Kit's *placement* was never the problem — the boss stopped on the armed slot **99.4%** of the time — but the trigger roll passed only 35.3%, so `kit2` fired 0.12 times per win. The roll deliberately stays as the gate, because springing a trap cancels the boss's entire declared move, the strongest party-wide effect in the ruleset. **Lv2 was left at 5 on purpose** so the sim measured exactly one variable; the accepted consequence is that Lv2 Trap now upgrades damage (5 → 7) without improving its roll.
  - **Measured outcome of that change: almost nothing** — success 35.3% → 38.8%, `kit2` 0.12 → 0.13 pts/win, against a theoretical near-doubling (d6 6+ = 16.7%, 5+ = 33.3%). Cause: Kit's Skill Improvement passive already discounts the target on every miss, and instrumenting the rolls actually faced showed **~26% of them happen at target 4+ or lower regardless of the base**. The real difficulty dial for Trap is the passive, not `rollBaseTarget`, which makes the base a blunt lever — worth recording so nobody re-tries it expecting more.
  - **`kit2` raised 1 → 2 points.** With frequency effectively locked by the passive, point value is the only lever left. Its share of Kit's score doubled (2% → 4% altruistic, 1% → 2% competitive) and Kit's competitive win share went 18.6% → 22.8%.
  - **`vera3` reverted to requiring Meteor** ("survive, having landed at least one Meteor") instead of v0.3.7's "survive, having landed any 14+ damage hit". The generic threshold let a fully-charged Fireball satisfy it, which made it overlap `vera1` and let Liora compound; Meteor is the ⏱7 wind-up the whole "protect me while I cast" fantasy is actually about. Fires 2.15 → 1.35 per win, pulling Liora from 33.2% → 20.1%. Implemented as `Fighter.landedMeteorThisBattle` (renamed back from `landedBigHitThisBattle`); interpreted as *Meteor connected for >0 damage*, not merely declared, so it cannot be satisfied by casting an empty Meteor at the end of a battle.
  - **Result** (3,000 games per tier): competitive play (hard bots) went from a 14.6pp spread to **9.9pp — Eric 30.0%, Luna 27.0%, Kit 22.8%, Liora 20.1%**, every character inside 20–30%. Against v0.3.6's original 32.1pp spread, the worst gap has fallen from 4.6× to **1.5×**.
  - **Known limitation, unchanged**: under purely altruistic play (medium bots, which by design never consult `scoreConditionBonus`) Liora sits at 11.2%, because `vera2` fires **0.00 times** there — a bot that cannot see score conditions will never bank mana for one. That figure is a property of the measuring tool rather than of human play, but it does mean Liora's kit only rewards a player who deliberately pursues it.
  - `npm test` 192/192 passing, `npm run typecheck` clean, `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` updated to the new Trap threshold.

- **v0.3.7 — score-condition rebalance: universal Last Shot bonus + rebuilt personal conditions** — driven by measurement, not taste. A 3,000-game sim of the v0.3.6 build showed win share was wildly uneven against a fair 25%: **Liora 40.9%, Luna 32.4%, Kit 17.9%, Eric 8.8%** — Liora won 4.6× as often as Eric. Three root causes, all measured, all fixed here. Full numbers in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md`.
  - **Last Shot is now a universal +2 for whoever lands the killing blow** (`LAST_SHOT_POINTS`/`LAST_SHOT_CONDITION_ID` in `src/content/characters.ts`, awarded in `scoring.ts`'s `onPlayerDealtDamage`). It was previously a *personal* condition worth 3 points that only Eric (`matt2`) and Liora (`vera2`) owned, so Kit and Luna scored nothing at all for the identical act — even though `state.lastShotCounts` already tracked it for everyone as a tie-break. Deliberately kept out of `CHARACTERS[].score`: it belongs to no one character, and the end-of-game breakdown groups it alongside `timeBonus`.
  - **Dead conditions replaced.** `matt3` ("end below 5 HP, alive") fired **0.13 times per win** and actively fought Berserk — it wanted low HP at the final frame while Berserk wants low HP *while attacking*, and a teammate's heal broke both. It is now "dropped below half HP at some point but never died" (3pts), latched via a new `Fighter.everDroppedBelowHalfThisBattle` so a later heal cannot erase the beating he took. `matt2` becomes "Guard absorbs a hit aimed at an ally" (2pts, per occurrence) — Eric's entire protector role previously scored *nothing* despite Guard being declared ~1,800 times per 3,000 games. Scored on the redirect itself via a new `onGuardRedirected` hook at `applyBossDamageToFighter`, so soaking a hit down to 0 counts (same rule Counter Attack already uses).
  - **"Free" survival conditions trimmed.** At a 91.5% win rate, `vera3` ("don't die") fired in 82% of battles and `luna3` ("nobody died") in 70%, together the two largest payouts in the game for doing very little. `luna3` drops 3→2 points; `vera3` now additionally requires she landed a 14+ damage hit that battle (new `Fighter.landedBigHitThisBattle`, latched off the same `VERA_BIG_HIT_DAMAGE` threshold `vera1` uses — one bar, two conditions). `kit3`'s bar goes 5→8 attacks, since Multi Shot lands 3 attacks from a single declare and the old bar cleared itself in 92% of battles.
  - **`kit2` (trap triggers) deliberately left unchanged** at the user's request. Flagging it honestly: it remains the weakest condition in the game at **0.12 fires per win** (1% of Kit's score), because it needs the boss to stop exactly on the slot *and* pass a 6+ roll.
  - **Two iterations were measured and reverted**: `vera2` was first written as "spend all 3 mana", which fired **exactly 0.00 times per win** — mana only comes from spending whole turns on Aura Charge and nobody ever banks three. Lowered to 2 (`VERA_CHARGED_CAST_MANA`). `luna2` was raised 1→2 to compensate for Luna's near-zero Last Shot rate, then reverted after it pushed her to a 38.8% win share.
  - **Tooling/bot fixes this exposed**: `tools/balance.ts` silently dropped the new `lastShot` payout from its per-character totals (it can't be attributed by `conditionId` alone, same as `timeBonus`) — now both route through the player's character, and the tool takes a bot tier as `argv[3]`. Crucially, **only the `hard` bot consults `scoreConditionBonus`** — `medium` plays purely for the party — so conditions needing deliberate setup are invisible under the default sim. `scoreConditionBonus` itself was updated for the new conditions (the old Liora "Meteor finish" nudge chased a condition that no longer exists) and its weights kept at ~1-2: tried at 3 and Liora's win share hit 84.9% on bot weighting alone, measuring the heuristic rather than the design.
  - **Result** (4,000 games each): under competitive play (hard bots, the closer analogue to humans playing for their own conditions) win share went from a 32.1pp spread to **15.1pp — Liora 33.3%, Eric 26.3%, Luna 22.1%, Kit 18.2%**, i.e. the worst gap fell from 4.6× to 1.8×. Under purely altruistic play (medium bots) the spread is 21.0pp. The two tiers disagree about Liora specifically (14.3% altruistic vs 33.3% competitive) because every one of her conditions now rewards deliberate setup — a property of the design, not a bug, but worth knowing.
  - `npm test` 192/192 passing (25 new tests covering the universal Last Shot for all four characters, the Guard-redirect hook, the charged-cast threshold, and every rewritten end-of-battle condition), `npm run typecheck` clean.

- **v0.3.5 — death/Multi Shot lifecycle hardening + fixed Blessing duration**

  - Kit's death immediately removes every remaining scheduled Multi Shot hit. Hits already resolved remain; revival cannot resume the cancelled action. Each hit still receives buffs and counts as an attack separately by design.

  - Removed Guard's obsolete `wardAtk` state/path and updated `GAME_DESIGN.md`, `RULINGS.md`, and generated skill text with the current rulings: ⚡ actions may resolve even past slot 0; Skill Improvement intentionally persists across bosses; difficulty remains unchanged for now.

- **v0.3.4 — immediate-resolve skills (⚡) + Multi Shot death-stop fix** — follow-up to v0.3.3's redesign. Two user-requested changes:
  - **7 skills now resolve at declare, not at the caster's next visit**: Slash, Power Strike (Eric), Quick Shot, Sharp Shooting (Kit), Air Push (Liora), Hitting, Aura Smite (Luna). New `SkillDef.immediate` flag in `src/content/characters.ts`. The pawn still walks its full ⏱ exactly as before — only *when* the damage (and, for Sharp Shooting, its weak-point roll) lands changed, from "next visit" to "right now."

A new `PendingAction.resolved` flag, set only when declareSkill actually applied an immediate effect, tells `resolveFighterPending` there's nothing left to do at the pawn's next visit — it just frees the pawn. This is deliberately *not* keyed off `SkillDef.immediate` directly, so any pending action built by hand (bypassing declareSkill) still resolves the old way at visit time; one existing test (`criticalFixes.test.ts`) relies on exactly that fallback and needed no changes.
    - `dealAttackFor`/`resolveAttackHits`/`resolveAttackRoll` extracted as shared functions so the immediate path (declareSkill) and the resolve-delayed path (resolveFighterPending, still used by Dax's Flurry/Focus and every other skill) run through identical damage math — no duplicated logic.
    - UI: a ⚡ badge now appears on the skill button face (`DecisionPanel.tsx`), the skill card and "already used" pending indicator (`HeroDetailModal.tsx`), the compact party status bar (`PartyStatBar.tsx`), and the tutorial's skill listing (`TutorialScreen.tsx`), plus a short caveat added to the tutorial's core-loop paragraph in both languages.
  - **Multi Shot (Kit) stops dead the instant its owner dies**: `processScheduledHitsAtMarker` now checks the caster is still alive before firing each early hit — previously it fired unconditionally regardless of the caster's state (a "fire and forget" design choice from v0.3.3 that the user flagged as wrong). A hit whose owner has died logs a wasted 0-dmg `RESOLVE_ATTACK` instead of dealing damage. Checked live at each hit's own marker tick rather than locked in at declare, so a revival landing in time before a later hit would let the skill resume — same philosophy as Berserk's resolve-time HP check.
  - Rewrote the now-invalid parts of `skills.test.ts` (the Berserk block's declare/resolve timing assumptions no longer hold once Power Strike/Slash are immediate; the Skill Improvement block needed its controlled dice moved from `resolveFighterPending` into `declareSkill`) and added new coverage: a dedicated "Immediate skills" describe block locking in the resolved-flag contract (including the hand-built-pending fallback), and a Multi Shot death-stop test.
  - `npm test` 169/169 passing, `npm run typecheck` clean, `vite build` succeeds, and verified live in-browser: boss HP visibly drops before the marker ticks for an immediate skill, the DECLARE log line precedes its own RESOLVE_ATTACK, and Multi Shot's 3 staggered hits still land correctly through the refactored shared helpers.

## 2026-08-13

- **v0.3.2 — declare the ①②③ skill role template as a rule, and rebuild Eric to fit it** — full design rationale in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §8.0 + the Eric section, every edge-case ruling in `docs/RULINGS.md` §7, and the complete measurement trail (including two failed versions of Guard) in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md` ("Role template + Eric rework").
  - **The template** (`docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §8.0): every character's three skills fill the same three roles — ① attack, ② support (never a direct attack), ③ signature. Checking the existing roster against it found the template already described **3 of the 4 characters exactly, with zero number changes** — Kit, Liora and Luna only needed their `skills` arrays reordered. It also correctly flags Mira (still out of the draft pool) as having no real slot ③, which matches what the sim already said about her.
  - **Why it's worth a rule, not just a habit:** §4.4 makes all information public, so planning a combo means reading the other three players' kits — 9 cards to scan for "who can help me" becomes 3 (everyone's slot ②). It also forces every character to have something to offer, which fills the §8 relationship matrix from 5 of 12 cells to 9.
  - **Eric was the only character who broke it** — Slash and Berserk both sit in slot ①, leaving ② empty. So the whole content change is one character:
    - **Berserk folded into Slash** as a damage tier (⏱4, 6 damage, or 11 while HP ≤ 5, checked at resolve). The declare-time gate is gone. A Luna heal arriving mid-flight now *downgrades* the hit 11 → 6 instead of wasting the whole action — the §5.5 Eric/Luna conflict survives in a form that doesn't delete a turn. Supporting datum: Berserk was declared only 425 times in ~20,000 Eric turns for 2.22 damage per declare against a printed 11, i.e. ~80% of them were being wasted. Slash's boosted number must stay above 10 forever (`matt1` scores ">10 in one hit"); pinned by a test.
    - **New skill: Guard** (⏱5) — redirects all damage aimed at one ally onto Eric, reduced by 4, and gives that ally +3 attack, until Eric's next turn. New `guard` SkillKind, state on `battle.guard` (same shape as Blessing's `partyBuff`). Against an AoE Eric takes both shares. Guard and Counter Attack can never overlap, so they're a real choice rather than a combo.
  - **Two findings that forced Guard's shape, both measured:** a *pure* redirect (no mitigation, no buff) dropped win rate 57.3% → 16.7%, because redirecting damage doesn't reduce it — it concentrates it on one 16 HP body, so `luna3` ("nobody died") fell 0.94 → 0.54 fires/game and total boss damage *rose*. And even with mitigation, a zero-damage support card can't pay its own ⏱ in this ruleset: every other character's slot ② feeds the damage economy, and Eric's was the only one producing nothing, costing the party ~11 damage/battle it doesn't have (§10). Both lessons are written into §8.0 as rules for future characters.
  - **`bossMoveTargets()` extracted** (`src/engine/clock/bossAI.ts`) so the boss's own resolvers and the bots' Guard heuristic read target selection from one function — the Set Trap slot bug of 2026-08-11 came from computing the same rule in two places.
  - Result (`npm run balance -- 3000`): win rate 57.3% → **54.4%**, boss clears 89/83/57% → **92/77/54%**, Aurelius armor broke ≥1× 39.6% → **60.4%**, hits ≥25 damage (the §8 stacked-combo proxy) **20 → 263**.
