# Changelog

Human-readable log of changes to this project, newest first. Add an entry here whenever you commit — whether the change was made by Claude Code or by hand — so anyone picking up the project can see what happened without digging through `git log`.

## 2026-08-13

- **v0.3.2 — declare the ①②③ skill role template as a rule, and rebuild Matt to fit it** — full design rationale in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §8.0 + the Matt section, every edge-case ruling in `docs/RULINGS.md` §7, and the complete measurement trail (including two failed versions of Guard) in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md` ("Role template + Matt rework").
  - **The template** (`docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §8.0): every character's three skills fill the same three roles — ① attack, ② support (never a direct attack), ③ signature. Checking the existing roster against it found the template already described **3 of the 4 characters exactly, with zero number changes** — Kit, Vera and Luna only needed their `skills` arrays reordered. It also correctly flags Mira (still out of the draft pool) as having no real slot ③, which matches what the sim already said about her.
  - **Why it's worth a rule, not just a habit:** §4.4 makes all information public, so planning a combo means reading the other three players' kits — 9 cards to scan for "who can help me" becomes 3 (everyone's slot ②). It also forces every character to have something to offer, which fills the §8 relationship matrix from 5 of 12 cells to 9.
  - **Matt was the only character who broke it** — Slash and Berserk both sit in slot ①, leaving ② empty. So the whole content change is one character:
    - **Berserk folded into Slash** as a damage tier (⏱4, 6 damage, or 11 while HP ≤ 5, checked at resolve). The declare-time gate is gone. A Luna heal arriving mid-flight now *downgrades* the hit 11 → 6 instead of wasting the whole action — the §5.5 Matt/Luna conflict survives in a form that doesn't delete a turn. Supporting datum: Berserk was declared only 425 times in ~20,000 Matt turns for 2.22 damage per declare against a printed 11, i.e. ~80% of them were being wasted. Slash's boosted number must stay above 10 forever (`matt1` scores ">10 in one hit"); pinned by a test.
    - **New skill: Guard** (⏱5) — redirects all damage aimed at one ally onto Matt, reduced by 4, and gives that ally +3 attack, until Matt's next turn. New `guard` SkillKind, state on `battle.guard` (same shape as Blessing's `partyBuff`). Against an AoE Matt takes both shares. Guard and Counter Attack can never overlap, so they're a real choice rather than a combo.
  - **Two findings that forced Guard's shape, both measured:** a *pure* redirect (no mitigation, no buff) dropped win rate 57.3% → 16.7%, because redirecting damage doesn't reduce it — it concentrates it on one 16 HP body, so `luna3` ("nobody died") fell 0.94 → 0.54 fires/game and total boss damage *rose*. And even with mitigation, a zero-damage support card can't pay its own ⏱ in this ruleset: every other character's slot ② feeds the damage economy, and Matt's was the only one producing nothing, costing the party ~11 damage/battle it doesn't have (§10). Both lessons are written into §8.0 as rules for future characters.
  - **`bossMoveTargets()` extracted** (`src/engine/clock/bossAI.ts`) so the boss's own resolvers and the bots' Guard heuristic read target selection from one function — the Set Trap slot bug of 2026-08-11 came from computing the same rule in two places.
  - Result (`npm run balance -- 3000`): win rate 57.3% → **54.4%**, boss clears 89/83/57% → **92/77/54%**, Aurelius armor broke ≥1× 39.6% → **60.4%**, hits ≥25 damage (the §8 stacked-combo proxy) **20 → 263**. The ~3pp win-rate drop is the intended price of Matt trading an attack card for a support card; Somnivar's 83 → 77% is also intended, since Guard at ⏱5 steps into the tax bracket Berserk just vacated.
  - **Known open issues, not fixed this pass** (detailed in BALANCE_NOTES): Vera's score lead widened (spread 2.9 → 5.4) and lowering Guard's buff was tested and doesn't fix it — the root is her own score conditions; Matt's score conditions still only measure attacking, so he has nothing rewarding his slot-② role the way kit1/luna1/luna2 do; Counter Attack is declared less now that Matt has a second defensive option; Guard may blunt Aurelius's "target the score leader" catch-up mechanic (logged as §11 risk #9 with two prepared levers).
  - `npm test` 157/157 passing (added 8 tests covering Slash's tier and Guard's redirect, mitigation, ward buff, expiry, guardian death, self-guard rejection, and counter-attribution), `npm run typecheck` clean, and the human decision panel verified in-browser: Guard's ally picker excludes the caster, both ends of the link show status badges, and a guarded Vera's Fireball correctly resolved for 11 (5 base + 3 Blessing + 3 Guard).

- **Bump package version 0.3.0 → 0.3.1** — `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, and the README title. Left every other `v0.3.0` reference in the codebase alone (comments like `// v0.3.0 "clock" ruleset` in `src/engine/index.ts`, `tools/balance.ts`, `src/content/characters.ts`, etc., plus `docs/RULINGS.md`'s title) — those name the *ruleset era* this whole engine was built for (distinguishing it from the cut 0.1.0/0.2.0 content), not the software's release version, and bumping them would misrepresent history.
- **Fold `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`'s own revision counter into the package version** — it had been numbering rule-change rounds independently as v0.4.1/v0.4.2/v0.4.3 since before this session, out of sync with `package.json`. Renamed v0.4.1→v0.3.0.1, v0.4.2→v0.3.0.2, v0.4.3→v0.3.1 (matching today's package bump above) everywhere those labels appear: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`, `docs/RULINGS.md`, `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md`, `tests/trapSlots.test.ts`, `src/engine/clock/skills.ts`. Going forward, a design-rule round that doesn't get its own package bump gets a `.N` suffix on the current package version; one that coincides with a package bump (like today's) just uses that version directly.

- **v0.3.1 — equal-start rebalance: fix low action counts, realign hero speed, toughen Vera** (`caf0a52`) — full rationale, rejected alternatives, and sim data in `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md` ("Equal-start rebalance — 2026-08-13"), full rule-text update in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` (marked v0.3.1 throughout). Starting problem: staggered hero start slots (20/22/23) meant real (resolved) actions per player per battle averaged only ~3.3, with a quarter of player-battles getting ≤2 — too few to feel the dice ladder or cross-player combos land more than once. Changes, all in `src/content/characters.ts` / `src/content/bosses3.ts` / `src/engine/clock/scoring.ts`:
  - All 4 heroes + all 3 bosses now start at clock slot 23 (was Matt/Vera 20, Kit 23, Luna 22, boss 22) — no engine change needed, §4.1's existing player-before-boss tiebreak handles the new stacking.
  - Boss HP +20% across the board (Ragorath 76→91, Somnivar 80→96, Aurelius 88→106) to compensate for the stronger, more synchronized party the equal start produces — swept +15/20/25%, +20% was the point where action count actually grew without win rate collapsing.
  - ⏱ realignment to match the stated character concept (Kit fastest, Matt/Luna medium, Vera slowest): Counter Attack ⏱5→4, Twin Shot ⏱5→4, Smite ⏱3→4 (dmg 4→6, lv2 6→8). Quick Shot and all of Vera's skills deliberately left untouched — see BALANCE_NOTES for why (Quick Shot ⏱2 was tried and reverted for making Kit's score run away; ManaCharge and Fireball changes were tried and reverted for other reasons).
  - Hero HP: Vera 8→11 (revive 4→6), Kit 12→13 (revive 6→7), Luna 12→13 (revive 6→7) — Vera specifically was dying to nearly every boss's hardest single hit even with her shield active.
  - `vera1` score condition threshold 15→14 damage (a fully-charged Fireball now qualifies on its own). `vera2` broadened from "Last Shot with Meteor" to "Last Shot with any skill," points cut 4→3.
  - Result (`npm run balance -- 2000`): win rate 43.8%→57.3%, boss clears 89/76/44%→89/83/57%, real actions/player ~3.3→~3.9, Vera deaths/battle ~0.50→~0.37-0.42.
  - **Known open issues, not fixed this pass** (all detailed in BALANCE_NOTES): Vera still wins ~43% of games (should be ~25%) and the cause hasn't been isolated yet; Somnivar's ⏱5+ tax now only catches 2 of the 4 skills it used to (Twin Shot/Counter moved to ⏱4); Luna's 3 skills are now all ⏱4 with no internal fast/slow choice.
  - Updated `tests/clockWalk.test.ts` (stacking order at the new shared start slot) and `tests/scoreConditions.test.ts` (new vera1/vera2 numbers) to match — `npm test` passes 146/146, `npm run typecheck` clean.

## 2026-08-11

- **Add Dax and Mira: a 6-character roster makes the draft matter** (`f256026`) — a 4-player table drafting from exactly 4 characters had no real choice, since the last picker just got whatever was left. `runDraft()` already iterated `CHAR_IDS` generically, so growing the pool to 6 needed zero engine changes; Dax (Duelist) and Mira (Elementalist) reuse only skill kinds the engine already handles.
- **Add synthesized sound effects** (`d17a794`) — clock tick, hits, deaths, victory/defeat.
- **Rebalance Luna's Heal condition; fix score-point duplication bug** (`82ba95c`) — extended `tools/balance.ts` to report each score condition's fire rate and points *per won game*, which surfaced a real bug: `scoring.ts` hardcoded point values separately from `CHARACTERS[charId].score[n].points` (what the UI displays), so a rebalance could silently make the UI lie about a condition's worth. Added `scorePoints(conditionId)` as the single source of truth.
- **Show where a skill will land before you declare it** (`a6637bd`) — a skill you were still choosing didn't show its landing slot the way declared actions already did. Skill buttons now show "⏱N → resolves at slot M" and flag in red when the clock would run out before it resolves.
- **Teach medium/hard bots to time weak point and Blessing together** (`fd8785d`) — Aurelius's armor only breaks above a >12-post-armor hit, which no single character can reach alone, but bots scored only their own pending action in isolation so the combo never happened (0 of 1500 sim games ever logged a ≥25 dmg hit). `comboSynergyBonus()` now reads teammates' declared pending actions and the boss's rolled next move to reward timing weak point + Blessing under a teammate's big attack.
- **Add end-game breakdown so a loss explains itself** (`6cbed8c`) — end screens stated only the outcome, not whether you were one hit short or nowhere close. Both screens now show the final battle's boss HP bar with a "short by N HP (P%)" line, plus per-player damage/healing/deaths, derived from the battle log with no engine or save-format change.
- **Enforce Set Trap's ⏱-window rule for human players** (`8ad94c7`) — the v0.3.0.2 redesign restricts Set Trap to slots inside its own ⏱ window, but the window was computed in two places: bots read the correct list, while the human decision panel offered every free slot below the marker, unvalidated. `legalTrapSlots()` is now the single source, consumed by both `buildDeclareOptions()` and the UI, and enforced in `declareSkill()`.
- **Fix skipped boss-defeat screen on 0-EXP battle transitions** (`976f70b`) — defeating a non-final boss with the clock at 0/1 remaining grants 0 EXP to everyone, so `runExpPlacement` had no one to ask and never yielded, and the engine fell straight through to the next boss within the same `gen.next()` call, skipping the outgoing battle's result popup. `GameSession.revealNewEvents()` now drains any leftover events on the outgoing battle before switching over.
- **Add win/lose backdrop art to end-game screens** (`b4af3ae`) — `ScoringScreen` now shows the arena at full bleed with a warm golden glow (victory feel); `AllLoseScreen` shows the boss that beat the party as a dark, vignetted backdrop (defeat feel), instead of both rendering on flat black.
